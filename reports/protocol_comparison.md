# Protocol Comparison

## How To Read This Comparison

The five protocols should not be grouped together as if they were the same kind of "RWA product." They represent different ways of bringing real-world assets onchain:

1. Ondo designs crypto-native tokenized Treasury yield products.
2. BlackRock BUIDL represents an institutional liquidity fund tokenized through Securitize.
3. Franklin BENJI represents shares of a U.S.-registered money market fund using blockchain-integrated recordkeeping.
4. Maple represents onchain institutional credit and cash management pools.
5. Centrifuge represents tokenization infrastructure for asset pools, private credit, structured credit, and fund products.

## Key Takeaways

1. Tokenized Treasuries are not one uniform product type. OUSG, USDY, BUIDL, and BENJI all connect to Treasury-like yield, but their legal structures and investor rights differ.
2. Traditional asset managers and crypto-native platforms are approaching RWA from different directions. BlackRock and Franklin start from regulated fund products, while Ondo starts from onchain product design and distribution.
3. Maple and Centrifuge broaden the RWA discussion beyond Treasuries. Maple is closer to institutional credit, while Centrifuge is closer to structured finance and securitization infrastructure.
4. The most important research question is not simply "what is the APY?" but "what does the token represent, who owns the underlying assets, how does redemption work, and where does the risk sit?"

## Summary

The protocol comparison shows that RWA is not a single product category. Ondo, BUIDL, BENJI, Maple, and Centrifuge each bring real-world assets onchain through different structures. Ondo uses crypto-native token design to distribute Treasury-like yield through products such as OUSG and USDY. BUIDL represents BlackRock's institutional liquidity fund tokenized through Securitize, while BENJI represents shares of Franklin Templeton's U.S.-registered money market fund using blockchain-integrated recordkeeping. Maple shifts the focus from Treasuries to institutional credit, where yield depends on borrower interest, underwriting, collateral, and pool liquidity. Centrifuge is closest to structured finance, providing infrastructure for issuers to create tokenized pools with share classes, investor restrictions, and pool-specific cash-flow rules. This comparison highlights why RWA analysis must focus on product structure, legal claims, redemption mechanics, and risk allocation rather than treating all tokenized assets as the same.
